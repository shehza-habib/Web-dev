Quaid-e-Azam

Father of Nation

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah is the revered founder of Pakistan, celebrated as a brilliant lawyer, a visionary statesman,
and the Baba-i-Qaum (Father of the Nation). Through his unwavering dedication, matchless political intellect, and commitment
to justice, he successfully led the All-India Muslim League to secure an independent homeland for the Muslims of the subconti-
nent on August 14, 1947.

Early Life Education

  • Birth: Born on December 25, 1876, at Wazir Mansion in Karachi (then part of the Bombay Presidency, British India) into a promi-
    nent mercantile family. His father was Jinnahbhai Poonja and his mother was Mithibai
  • Schooling:Received his early education at the Sindh Madressatul Islam and the Christian Missionary Society High School in Karachi,
    followed by a brief period at the Cathedral and John Connon School in Bombay.
  • Higher Studies: Traveled to England at age 16 and enrolled at Lincoln's Inn. At just 19, he became the youngest British Indian to be
    called to the Bar.
