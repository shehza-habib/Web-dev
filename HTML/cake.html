Chocalate Cake

A simple and delicious chocalte cake recepie for brginner.
Perfect for Birthday Celebration!
Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time:30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cup flour
  • 2 cup sugar>
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp cinamon(optional)
  • 1/2 cup coca powder
  • 1 cup milk

Steps

  1. Pre heat the oven over 20 minutes.
  2. Slowly mix the sugar, powder and flour.
  3. Add milk and egg and stir until it become smmoth.
  4. 1 tsp cinamon(optional).
  5. 1/2 cup coca powder.
  6. Baked for 30 minutes or until toothpluck comes out clean.

Tips

For extra Flavor add extra Chocalate chipbefore baking
You can also serve by Vanilla ice cream for a delicioues comobo!
